# Privacy notice: Ikon Platform Validation

The Validation app is an **internal test app** run by Ikon AI to check that the Ikon platform works. It is not a product and is not offered to the public. If you reached it by accident, close the page; nothing you do here is used for anything but testing the platform.

## Who is responsible

Ikon AI runs this app and the platform it is built on. Contact: [support@ikonai.com](mailto:support@ikonai.com).

## What is processed

- **Sign-in.** Visitors browse as guests. If you choose to sign in, the platform receives your account id, name and email from the sign-in provider you picked.
- **What you type or record.** Text, files, microphone, camera and screen input you send in a test are processed to run that test. Some tests send it to AI model providers.
- **Consent answers.** What you allowed or declined under "Consent" is stored against your user id so the app can respect it.
- **Technical logs.** Connection and error logs kept by the platform for operating it.

## How long

Test data is cleared regularly. Your consent answers and account are kept until you withdraw them or delete the account.

## Your choices

- Withdraw consent at any time on the Identity tab.
- Delete your account from the Identity tab. Deletion runs after a grace period during which you can cancel it.
- Ask us anything about your data at the address above.
